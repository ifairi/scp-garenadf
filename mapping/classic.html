Make the next move clear.

Choose a battlefield or upload your own map. Select a tool and draw directly on the map.

  • Select: click an annotation to move it, change its color or size, or delete it.
  • Text & markers: edit the label in the toolbar, then click the map to place it.
  • Eraser: drag across an annotation to remove the whole annotation.
  • Navigate: use Pan, hold Space and drag, or drag with two fingers. Pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom.
  • Undo / redo: Ctrl or Cmd + Z / Shift + Z. Delete removes your selection. Escape cancels the current stroke.
  • Save strategy: download an editable file including the map. Open it here to continue. Export PNG creates an image at the map’s original size.

Your latest strategy is saved in this browser when storage is available. Download a strategy file to keep a separate copy or share it with your squad.
